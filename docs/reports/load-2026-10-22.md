# Load and latency, 2026-10-22

Measured by `./scripts-load.sh` (TST-03, [#166](https://github.com/AY2627S1-CS3219-P30/foc-app/issues/166))
on a stack of its own, from commit `b44b42b`.

- **Load:** 100 concurrent virtual users (k6) in each scenario, for 60 s, each
  pausing 1 s between requests, as a person does.
- **Data:** 100 students who sign in, 1,000 active suppliers, 10,000 wallets.
- **Machine:** Darwin arm64; Docker with 12 CPUs and 7.7 GiB. The virtual users and the stack share it; nothing else ran beside them.
- The User Service's sign-in limits are raised on this stack only (`compose.load.yaml`).
