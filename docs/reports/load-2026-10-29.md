# Load and latency, 2026-10-29

Measured by `./scripts-load.sh` (TST-03, [#166](https://github.com/AY2627S1-CS3219-P30/foc-app/issues/166))
on a stack of its own, from commit `277fe69`.

- **Load:** 100 concurrent virtual users (k6) in each scenario, for 60 s, each
  pausing 1 s between requests, as a person does.
- **Data:** 100 students who sign in, 1,000 active suppliers, 10,000 wallets.
- **Machine:** Darwin arm64; Docker with 12 CPUs and 7.7 GiB. The virtual users and the stack share it; nothing else ran beside them.
- The User Service's sign-in limits are raised on this stack only (`compose.load.yaml`).

## Live updates (N1.4)

40 students each held 5 live-update streams
([ADR 0011](../adr/0011-live-updates.md)) straight to the Order Service while the scenarios above
ran, so their response times were measured with 200 streams open. Meanwhile an errand was
posted and accepted at a steady rate, and each accept was timed from its answer to the nudge on
the requester's stream ([#332](https://github.com/AY2627S1-CS3219-P30/foc-app/issues/332)).

| Check | Target | Measured | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Streams held open, each saying `ready` | all of them | 200 of 200 (40 students × 5) | Met |
| An accept reaches its requester's stream, p95 | < 1 s | 492 ms (p50 290 ms, max 503 ms; 60 accepts) | Met |
| Nudges lost: none within 5 s of the accept | 0 | 0 | Met |
| A sixth stream for one student | refused, `429` | 429 | Met |
| Errands the client could not post, see open, or accept | 0 | 0 of 60 | Met |

That transfers and returns requested before a restart complete without repair (CS-NFR3.1.2) is
checked by `./scripts-restart-check.sh`, which CI runs. The Order Service's targets (OS-NFR2) and
the share of credit steps recorded within five seconds (CS-NFR3.1.1) are ORD-12,
[#181](https://github.com/AY2627S1-CS3219-P30/foc-app/issues/181), on this harness.
